About Us

CJ Co Timepieces was born out of a deep passion for horology and a desire to make quality timepieces accessible to every Filipino. What started as a personal collection soon grew into a trusted watch dealership, offering carefully selected watches from some of the world's most respected brands.

We believe that a great watch is more than just a timekeeping device — it is a statement of character, taste, and dedication to craft. Every timepiece in our collection is handpicked to ensure that our customers receive nothing but the finest quality, whether they are first-time buyers or seasoned collectors.

At CJ Co Timepieces, customer trust is our top priority. We are committed to providing an honest, transparent, and enjoyable buying experience for every customer. Whether you are looking for a everyday timepiece or a special gift for someone you love, we are here to help you find the perfect watch.
